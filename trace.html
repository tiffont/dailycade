How to play

  1. 1Start at the glowing dotTouch it and trace along the line of cubes. Lift and you can pick up where you left off
  2. 2Stay on the lineThe closer your line passes each cube, the more points. Up to 70 a shape
  3. 3Be quickFinish before the white mark on the timer for up to 30 more. 100 a shape, 1,000 a game
  4. 4Watch it fall apartOnly cubes you trace well break off and fall. The ones you miss stay stuck on the board

Cutting corners doesn’t pay: every cube is scored by how close your line came to it. Shapes 9 and 10 turn while you trace.
