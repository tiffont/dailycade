How to play

  1. 1Pick your slotsAll 10 boards, top to bottom. Tap Random to pick for you.
  2. 2Tap DropThe ball starts from a hidden spot and falls through every board
  3. 3Score where it landsYour pick is 100, the slots beside it 25, the rest 10
  4. 4Three drops a gameYour score is all three added up. The Daily challenge uses the same boards for everyone.

Watch for

  • 💣BombExplodes: score to 0, drop over
  • 10x10xScore times 10
  • SplitSplitOne ball becomes two
  • PortalPortalDrop again from the top
  • RewindRewindReplay the same board
  • TripTripSkip the next level
  • ½HalfScore cut in half
  • VoidVoidDrops straight through the next board
  • AimAimYou pick where the next drop starts
  • +1BonusReplay the board, your score keeps adding
  • MagnetMagnetPulls the ball into your 100
