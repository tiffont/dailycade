Privacy

Effective November 8, 2026

Dailycade is a small collection of free daily games. We collect as little as we can, we don't run ads, and we don't sell your information.

What we collect

  • Leaderboard scores. If you sign in to play a daily challenge, your score, how long you lasted and your display name are saved so they can appear on that game's leaderboard.
  • Game settings on your device. Your best score, sound settings and whether you've played today's game are kept in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

What we don't collect

No email list, no payment details, no location, no advertising or tracking cookies, and no cross-site tracking.

How it's used

Only to run the games: showing leaderboards, keeping you to one daily game, and remembering your settings.

Sharing

Leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players. Our games are hosted by a third-party platform that stores this data on our behalf. We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Your choices

  • Play without signing in and nothing is posted to a leaderboard.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove anything saved on your device.
  • Ask us to delete your leaderboard entries at any time through our contact page.

Children

Dailycade isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Do not sell or share my personal information

Effective November 8, 2026

Dailycade does not sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't run ads or use advertising trackers.

Because there's nothing to opt out of, you don't need to do anything. We also honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals from your browser automatically.

If you'd like to confirm what we hold about you or ask us to delete it, contact us at tiffont@gmail.com. See our privacy page for details.
