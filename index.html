Privacy

Effective November 25, 2026

Dailycade is a small collection of free daily games. We collect as little as we can, we don't run ads, and we don't sell your information.

Dailycade is in beta

Dailycade is an early, beta release. Games, features and this policy may change as we go, and we may reset daily challenges, scores or leaderboards while we fine-tune things. If something breaks or feels off, tap the Beta tag at the top of the homepage or Send feedback in any game, or email tiffont@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Leaderboard scores. When you finish a daily challenge, your score, a few details of the run and any initials you choose to enter are saved so they can appear on that game's leaderboard. Each device gets a random, anonymous player ID for this; there are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name or email.
  • Beta feedback. If you send feedback or tap 👍/👎 after a game, we store what you wrote, your rating, the game and page you were on, your device and browser type, and today's game and score. An email is only saved if you choose to add one, and is only used to reply to you. Feedback is visible only to us and is used only to improve the games.
  • Anonymous visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits and see which pages and games are used. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify you.
  • Game settings on your device. Your best score, sound settings and whether you've played today's game are kept in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Email updates

If you sign up for email updates, we save your email address and the date you signed up, and use it only to tell you about new games and big changes to Dailycade. We send these through Resend, our email provider, on our behalf. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove your address through our contact page.

What we don't collect

No payment details, no location, no advertising or tracking cookies, and no cross-site tracking.

How it's used

Only to run and improve the games: showing leaderboards, keeping you to one daily game, remembering your settings, and understanding which games people play.

Sharing

Leaderboard names and scores are visible to other players. The site is hosted by Vercel, and scores and game settings are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on our behalf. We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Your choices

  • Play single player and nothing is posted to a leaderboard. Leaving the initials box empty keeps your daily score on the leaderboard without a name.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove anything saved on your device.
  • Ask us to delete your leaderboard entries at any time through our contact page.

Children

Dailycade isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Do not sell or share my personal information

Effective November 13, 2026

Dailycade does not sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't run ads or use advertising trackers.

Because there's nothing to opt out of, you don't need to do anything. We also honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals from your browser automatically.

If you'd like to confirm what we hold about you or ask us to delete it, contact us at tiffont@gmail.com. See our privacy page for details.
