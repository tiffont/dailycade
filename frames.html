How to play

  1. 1Pick your spotTouch and hold the ball and move it anywhere in the box behind the line: left, right, forward or back
  2. 2Pull back and swingStill holding, pull the ball back toward you, then swing it up the lane and let go, like a real throw. Swing it over the line and it leaves your hand on its own. The faster the swing, the harder it rolls
  3. 3Bend your swipe to curveThe ball heads the way your swipe starts. Bend the swipe right or left and it hooks that way, late, near the pins. Slower balls hook more
  4. 4Hit the pocketStraight at the front pin leaves a split. Come in from the side and curve into the gap beside it to strike

Ten frames, two balls each. A strike scores 10 plus your next two balls, a spare 10 plus your next one. A perfect game is 300.
