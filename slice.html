How to play

  1. 1Swipe straight throughOne swipe per object, all the way across
  2. 2Cut it exactly in halfThe closer to 50 / 50, the more points. 100 each, 1,000 a game
  3. 3It gets harderThey start still, then spin, tumble and come faster
  4. 4Don't let it reach youA red glow means it's about to. Miss and it scores 0

Solid objects are scored by volume, so cut through the middle of the whole thing, not just what faces you.
