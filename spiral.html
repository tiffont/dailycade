How to play

  1. 1Hold the ball anywhereTouch the ball and move it anywhere in the bottom half of the screen. It leans more the closer it gets to an edge. Left of centre throws lefty, and the ball spins the other way
  2. 2Flick up to throwTouch anywhere, then swipe up the screen and let go. The faster the flick, the harder the throw
  3. 3Height and distanceA longer swipe lofts the ball higher; a short, quick one throws it flat. Speed and length together set where it comes down
  4. 4Bend to curveThe ball heads the way your swipe starts. Bend the swipe right or left and it curves that way in the air
  5. 5Throw a perfect spiralThey're rare. Make one very smooth swipe with no shake or kinks, flick it fast, and keep speeding up right through the release instead of easing off. A perfect spiral is worth 50 bonus points when it hits the target. A shaky swipe throws a wobbly ball that drifts and falls short

Ten throws. A bullseye shatters the orange centre for 100, and its pieces stay on the field. Inner ring 50, outer ring 25, off the rim 10. Each target sits at a random distance; from throw 6 it slides along the cable, and from throw 8 it bobs too.
